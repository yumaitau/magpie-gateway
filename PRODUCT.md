# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Administrators, policy editors, approvers and auditors managing an Australian AI gateway. They configure policies, trial enforcement, manage access and inspect decisions from desktop and laptop browsers. The portal also needs to work on a phone.

## Product Purpose

Magpie Gateway gives organisations visibility and control over AI traffic, including Australian DLP checks, approvals, runtime enforcement settings and per-action role permissions. Existing functionality and permission boundaries must remain usable throughout a visual redesign.

## Brand Personality

Australian, watchful and distinctive. The user explicitly requested an identity that reflects the Australian magpie and rejected the existing plain presentation. The bird must be recognisable through its black-and-white plumage, white nape and pale bill.

## Anti-references

The previous beige-and-green generic admin interface, anonymous dashboard cards, cartoon security mascots, and decorative effects that make operational controls harder to use.

## Design Principles

- Give the Australian magpie a visible, consistent role in the identity.
- Keep operational facts and controls clearer than decoration.
- Distinguish log-only trials from actual enforcement honestly.
- Preserve familiar navigation, labels and native form behaviour.
- Keep artwork and styling local; no third-party runtime asset requests.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain the existing axe checks for WCAG 2 A/AA, 2.1 A/AA and 2.2 AA. Preserve keyboard navigation, visible focus, skip links, semantic headings, labelled forms, reduced-motion support and responsive access to tables.

The user explicitly requires all features and the appearance of the built-in Agentgateway UI. Serve the version-matched native console, integrate Magpie governance, protect management APIs with per-action RBAC, and persist UI edits across deployments.

The product has one native UI. Account security, colleague invitations, custom roles, groups and every governance screen belong in that interface; do not link users into a separate legacy portal shell.
